Three products, live now

Here is what each one does and who it is for.

CallChloé

An AI phone secretary for real estate agents. Chloé picks up while you are with a client, so a missed call stops meaning a missed deal.

  • Answers at any hour, weekends and holidays included
  • Speaks English and French, and switches mid-call
  • Books appointments into Google Calendar during the call
  • Sends a text and email summary after every call
Visit callchloe.ai (opens in a new tab)

Tenaivo

A property management CRM for owners and operators. Calls, texts, emails and WhatsApp messages land in one ticket log, and nothing leaves it until the job is closed.

  • Every channel in one log
  • Requests answered in under 60 seconds
  • A real coordinator dispatches the vendor and follows the job to the invoice
  • French and English, around the clock
Visit tenaivo.com (opens in a new tab)

LayoffMath

A free severance calculator for people facing a layoff. Enter a few details and see how an offer compares before signing anything.

  • Covers all 50 US states and the Canadian provinces
  • About a minute, no account needed
  • Shows your score and the gap in your offer
  • Educational estimates, not legal advice
Visit layoffmath.com (opens in a new tab)

How we work

The same three steps, every time.

  1. Find a problem worth owning

    We look for work that businesses still lose track of: missed calls, untracked requests, numbers nobody checks.

  2. Build the product

    Design, code and launch happen in-house. AI goes where it beats making someone wait.

  3. Stay and run it

    We remain the operator after launch: support, billing and every improvement. Nothing gets handed off.

How we think

Five habits that shape every product.

Long-term
We build assets, not campaigns. Each product is meant to compound over years.
Design first
Good design is how a product earns trust, so it is never the last step.
Systems over tactics
Automated pipelines and clear workflows that remove friction and multiply output.
Measured
Load times, conversion, acquisition cost, retention. We track them and act on them.
Lean
Small team, strong tools, no bureaucracy.
